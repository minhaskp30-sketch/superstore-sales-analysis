# Sample Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Tableau dashboard providing a comprehensive view of sales performance, profitability trends, customer segmentation, and geographical distribution for the Sample Superstore dataset.

## Key Insights & Metrics
* **KPI Summary:** Tracks total sales ($2.33M), total profit ($292.3K), overall profit ratio (12.56%), total orders (10,194), and active customer base (804 customers).
* **Sales Trends Over Time:** Evaluates monthly and yearly revenue trajectories from 2021 through 2024 to identify seasonal peaks.
* **Category & Sub-Category Performance:** Technology leads overall sales ($840K), followed by Furniture ($755K) and Office Supplies ($732K), with sub-category profitability highlighting top contributors like Copiers and Phones.
* **Geographic Distribution:** State-by-state and city-level sales maps tracking high-volume hubs including California ($457.7K) and New York ($138.6K).
* **Customer Segment Breakdown:** Sales share analysis across Consumer (50% / $1.17M), Corporate ($0.72M), and Home Office ($0.44M) segments, alongside top individual customer revenue rankings.
